## Cosmetic, for later

### Suffering

The cat suffers when something is off or too many sessions are working. Cute is the wrapper: the suffering is red, visible and over the top, but only sometimes.

Levels:

1. **Uneasy:** heat "fair", 3 or more sessions working at once, or the battery close to the floor.
2. **Suffering:** eepy has paused (too hot, battery too hot, battery floor, a sensor it can't read), or 5 or more sessions are working at once.
3. **Fits:** at the suffering level only. About once a minute the cat loses it for a few seconds, then drops back to level 2.

It's only seen with the lid open: a pause with the lid closed sleeps the Mac and stops eepy.

Looks:

- Colour climbs from peach to orange to red. In a fit everything goes red (cat, cloud, gauges, dividers), with one flash of inverted colours as it starts.
- Sweat drops and steam rise instead of z's.
- The cat trembles, shaking a column left and right.
- Too hot: it melts, the bottom of the cat dripping down.
- A spiky scream bubble replaces the soft cloud.
- Eyes snap open: wide, crossed out, or spirals. These are edits to the owner's drawing, previewed first.
- Glitch: the scene tears, lines shoved sideways, stray dots.
- Cursed text: glitch marks stacked on the thought, like c̷a̸n̶'t̵ b̸r̴e̷a̶t̶h̷e̸. Centre it on the plain text's length, since the marks take no width.
- The tab title becomes `eepy · HELP`.

Says (each fits the cloud's 28 characters):

- "the fans scream. so do I"
- "I can taste the CPU"
- "my whiskers are melting"
- "7 Claudes. one lid. help"
- "they keep spawning…"
- "I am become toast"
- "the battery whispers to me"
- "let me sleep. LET ME SLEEP"
- "this is fine."

### Petting

- Press `p` in the eepy window and a few hearts float up instead of z's.
- Pet it about 5 times in a short while and it snaps: its fur bristles, it goes red and the cloud says "HSSSS". Three claw slashes then rip diagonally across the whole screen, panel included, for a few seconds.
- Then it sulks ("don't.") and ignores petting for a minute.
